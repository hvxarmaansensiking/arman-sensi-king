📱 Device setup checklist

Useful basics before changing sensitivity.

1

Choose a stable graphics and frame-rate setting your phone can maintain.

2

Keep touch controls comfortable and avoid changing every setting at once.

3

Try each preset in Training Ground, then make small adjustments.

4

Use the same settings for several practice sessions before judging them.

🔫 Popular Gun Notes

Settings can feel different for each weapon and play style.

💥

M1887

Close range: Practice a short upward drag after placing the crosshair near the target. Keep the movement controlled.

🎯

Desert Eagle

Precision: Focus on calm crosshair placement and deliberate taps instead of rushing each shot.

⚡

UMP

Tracking: Practice following a moving target smoothly; avoid over-dragging your aim.

🔥

M1014

Close range: Work on positioning and timing in Training Ground before changing sensitivity.

🦅

Woodpecker

Mid range: Use controlled aim adjustments and leave time between taps to reset your crosshair.

🔭

Sniper rifles

Long range: Try lower scope sensitivity if you frequently move past your target.

🔥 Headshot Tips & Tricks

Build consistency through practice

📍

Crosshair placement

Keep your crosshair around upper-body level as you move, then adjust carefully when aiming.

🧪

Change one thing

If aim feels too fast or slow, adjust one slider a little and test again.

⏱️

Short practice rounds

Use a few focused Training Ground rounds and take breaks if your hands feel tired.
